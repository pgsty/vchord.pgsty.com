Learn about the limits and restrictions you should know about your operations on VectorChord Cloud:
- [Free Tier](/cloud/pricing/price-plan/#free-tier): One account can create only **one** free tier cluster.
- [Project](/cloud/manage/project/): One account can belong to maximum **five** projects. 
- Does not support HA (High Availability) mode yet.
